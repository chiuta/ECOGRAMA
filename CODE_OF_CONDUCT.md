# Code of conduct

**English** · [Română](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ro.md) · [Français](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.fr.md) · [Italiano](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.it.md) · [Español](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.es.md) · [Português](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.pt.md) · [Deutsch](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.de.md) · [Magyar](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.hu.md)

Be respectful and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing and spam are not accepted. Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions and block participants who do not follow this. Report problems privately to alexio@trom.tf. ECOGRAMA follows the trade-free spirit: no tracking, no dark patterns, no hidden costs.

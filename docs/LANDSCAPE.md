# Related projects and where ECOGRAMA fits

*Checked with a limited web search on 13 November 2026. Facts change quickly: re-check before announcing or depending on anything below.*

- **cathar** — a pure-Rust audio-restoration toolkit and CLI (denoise, de-hum, de-click, de-clip, de-reverb, spectral repair): <https://docs.rs/crate/cathar/latest>. It covers *processing*; check its licence and API before deciding whether to depend on it, interoperate or contribute upstream (issue #10).
- **spek-rs** (MIT) — a spectrum *viewer* wrapping FFmpeg, not an editor.
- **Commercial references:** iZotope RX, Steinberg SpectraLayers (interactive spectral editing).
- **Gap targeted:** an open, interactive *spectrogram editor* (select, repair, undo) in Rust; we did not find one in a limited search.
